# AE/AF lock trick: voiceover script (for ElevenLabs)

Timed to `videos/eye/aeaf_lock_trick_1m44s.mp4`. Each line has a window; aim to finish inside it
(about 2.5 words a second, a relaxed pace). Times are mm:ss from the start of the video.
Music in the video is a bed at -17 LUFS; when the voice goes on, lower the music stem
(`videos/eye/audio/aeaf_lock_music.wav`) by 8 to 10 dB, or duck it under the voice.

| Window | On screen | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 - 0:05 | "Make your phone camera depend on your glasses." | Here's a neat trick: make your phone camera depend on your glasses. |
| 0:05 - 0:10 | "...and it takes pictures just like you see without them." | Then take the glasses away, and it takes pictures just like you see without them. |
| 0:10.5 - 0:14 | Step 1 card | First, point your phone at something far away. Six feet or more. |
| 0:14 - 0:16.5 | (phone pointed at the skyline) | *(pause, or:)* Anything in the distance works. |
| 0:16.5 - 0:20 | Step 2 card | Hold your glasses in front of the camera. I'm using a minus three lens. |
| 0:20 - 0:27 | (glasses go over the lens) | The camera is now looking through your prescription, so the picture stays sharp. |
| 0:27 - 0:30.5 | Step 3 card | Press and hold the screen until AE/AF LOCK appears. |
| 0:30.5 - 0:35.5 | ("FOCUS LOCKED") | That locks the focus right where your glasses put it. |
| 0:35.5 - 0:39 | Step 4 card | Now take the glasses away. The focus stays locked. |
| 0:39 - 0:43 | (glasses come off, zoom into the screen) | Watch the screen. Everything far away goes soft. |
| 0:43 - 0:46.5 | Step 5 card | Your camera now sees like you do without glasses. |
| 0:46.5 - 0:52.5 | (close-up on the blurry screen, photo taken) | Snap a photo, and show your friends exactly what you see. |
| 0:52.5 - 0:59.5 | "How a -3.00 nearsighted person sees" | This is how a minus three nearsighted person sees. With glasses... and without. |
| 0:59.5 - 1:07 | Wipe between the photos | Same view, same person. The only difference is the glasses. |
| 1:07 - 1:13 | "The lens moves the camera's focus" | Why does it work? A minus three lens pulls the camera's focus in close, to about thirty-three centimetres. |
| 1:13 - 1:18 | Lock, then the lens slides away | Lock it there, take the lens away, and the camera is nearsighted, just like you. |
| 1:18 - 1:28 | "Up close: clear. Far away: blurry." | So things up close stay sharp, and the farther away they are, the blurrier they get. |
| 1:28 - 1:37.5 | "It can't show astigmatism" | One catch: a camera can only blur evenly, so it can't show astigmatism, the "cyl" in your prescription. It works for nearsighted prescriptions. |
| 1:37.5 - 1:44 | "Show your friends how you really see." | Give it a try, and show your friends how you really see. |
