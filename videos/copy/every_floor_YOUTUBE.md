**Description**

```
Last time, a Roomba with no map drove every road in America at the speed of light. It didn't clean a thing. So this time: a modern robot vacuum, the kind that maps the room and cleans in neat rows 25 cm apart, moving at the speed of light. It cleans every floor of every building in New York City. Then every floor on Earth.

🧹 One Empire State Building (261,312 m², 102 floors): 1,045 km of rows, 3.5 milliseconds.
🧹 Every floor in New York (1,081,950 buildings, 535 million m²): 7.4 seconds. The rows would reach the Moon and back nearly three times.
🧹 Every floor on Earth (about 273 billion m², about 2.5 billion buildings): about 1 hour 8 minutes. An hour of vacuuming, 7 minutes getting from building to building. The rows, end to end: 1.09 billion km, farther than Jupiter is from the Sun.
🧹 The dustbin: about 82,000 tonnes of dust, half a billion bin-fulls. At 10 seconds per emptying, 173 years.
🧹 The physics: nothing with mass reaches light speed. At 99% of it, a 4 kg robot stopping at the end of a row releases about 10 Tsar Bombas. It makes about 273 billion U-turns.

What should it clean next? Tell me in the comments.
```
