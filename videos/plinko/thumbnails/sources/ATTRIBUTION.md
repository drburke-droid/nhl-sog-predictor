# Third-party image used in thumbnails A3, B3 and C3

`The_Price_Is_Right_Plinko_Home_Game_960px.jpg` is the 960 px preview of
"The Price Is Right Plinko Home Game" by TaurusEmerald, 8 April 2026,
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Price_Is_Right_Plinko_Home_Game.jpg
licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

`plinko_chaos_thumb_A3.png`, `B3` and `C3` adapt it: cropped, with the film's two simulated paths drawn on the
board's pegs (mapped onto the photo by a homography fitted to 96 detected pegs), rendered chips added
(`plinko/blender/render_thumb_chips.py`), prize labels and text beside it. They are therefore also licensed
CC BY-SA 4.0. The PLINKO name and logo on the board are trademarks of their owners; their appearance does not
imply endorsement.
