Actually interesting waiting room videos.

We're Calgary Vision Centre, an eye clinic in Calgary, Alberta. These are the videos we'd rather have on the screen while you wait: big "what if" questions worked out with real data, plus a few about the eyes doing the watching.

🌍 Light-speed maps: every road on Earth (it takes light 4 min 40 s), every flight, a Roomba driving every road in America
🎨 Scale you can see: coats of paint until a room disappears, a newspaper folded all the way to the Sun, 670 million mph next to a fighter jet
⏳ Simulations: everyone alive as grains of sand, chaos on a Plinko board, whether a whisk can ever fully mix
🔥 Fire and sightlines: signal fires around the world, the longest sightline on Earth
👁 Eyes: how thick glasses get, binocular vision dysfunction, a trick to show friends how you see without glasses

Got a "what if"? Leave it in the comments. The best ones become videos.
