# Assets

* `bmw27_cpu.blend`: the "bmw27" scene by Mike Pan, from Blender's benchmark
  (https://download.blender.org/demo/test/BMW27_2.blend.zip). Blender's benchmark content is public domain (CC0)
  (https://www.blender.org/news/introducing-blender-benchmark/). Only its car (collection "1M") is used; the badges are
  hidden.
* `barrel_03/`: "Barrel 03" from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com/a/barrel_03), CC0.
* `sign_100.png`: drawn by `props.sign_texture` (Inter Display Bold, SIL Open Font License).
