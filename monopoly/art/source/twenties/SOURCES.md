# 1920s Americana images: sources

There are 23 images. Each is an RGB JPEG with its long side at most 2400 px. Every image is a scan from the Library of Congress Prints & Photographs Division, and every one is on Wikimedia Commons, where we checked its public-domain status through the API (LicenseShortName "Public domain", plus the PD tag and categories).

- 3 images (`ac_miss_america_gorman_neptune_1922`, `ac_carnival_1922`, `ac_bathing_beauties_carnival_1922`) were downloaded from Commons, at the 3840 px standard thumbnail size.
- The other 20 were downloaded from the LOC master TIFF of the same scan, because Commons refused our generic User-Agent with HTTP 429 ("robot policy"). Each was then reduced to 2400 px. The LOC master is uncropped, so these files can show more of the plate border than the Commons copy.
- `games_children_checkers_1911` was cropped to remove the LOC colour-calibration strip.

## Rights notes to double-check before publishing

These are probably public domain, but the record is thinner than for the rest:

- `ac_hotel_traymore_1920`: the Commons tag (PD-old-70) is wrong, because Irving Underhill died in 1960. The claim depends on the print being published in 1920. Check the LOC rights statement for item 00650140.
- `jail_liquor_into_sewer_1921`, `ac_boardwalk_rolling_chairs_traymore`: photographer unknown. The claim depends on publication before 1931.
- `ac_miss_america_gorman_neptune_1922`: it carries PD-Bain and LOC "no known restrictions", but Commons also has a "copyright tag still required" maintenance flag on it.
