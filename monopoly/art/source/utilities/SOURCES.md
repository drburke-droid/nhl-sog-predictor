# Utilities (Electric Company and Water Works) images: sources

There are 14 images. Each is an RGB JPEG with its long side at most 2400 px. All 14 come from the Library of Congress Prints & Photographs Division and were downloaded from the LOC master TIFF (`tile.loc.gov`). Each TIFF was then reduced to 2400 px on its long side. Nothing came from Wikimedia Commons.

- The LOC masters are uncropped. Many show the glass-plate edge, plate numbers, or handwritten captions (Bain and National Photo Co.). Crop these out in the edit if you want a clean frame.
- `home_electric_iron_ge_1908.jpg` is only 1226x1536, because that is the full size of the LOC master. This is below the 1800 px target. Use it only as a small snapshot cutaway.
- The dates in the file names are approximate. The Date column gives the date exactly as LOC records it.

The "Public-domain basis" column quotes the item's LOC rights advisory word for word. The note after the semicolon is our reasoning.

## Rights notes

- The 8 Detroit Publishing Co. images and the 2 Bain News Service images are the strongest cases. These are commercial and press photographs published before 1931, and each LOC record says "No known restrictions on publication."
- There are 3 National Photo Company items: the lineman, the water main and the showroom. The National Photo Co. collection is widely treated as public domain, and LOC states "No known restrictions." Two of them, the water main and the showroom, are copy negatives dated only "between 1909 and 1932", so their records are thinner. If you want zero doubt, use them as short cutaways or replace them.
- None of the 14 images shows violence or an accident. We rejected Bain's "Edison's big generator, 12/10/14" (LOC 2014703602) because it shows debris from the December 1914 fire at Edison's West Orange works.

## Gaps

- We found no period interior of a big New York turbine hall: Hell Gate, Waterside and New York Edison all came up empty at LOC. The New York Edison material at LOC is either Joseph Pennell drawings or low-resolution copy prints. The Detroit Edison plant and the "Washington" dynamos stand in for it.
- We found no pumping-station interior. The Cincinnati "New pumping plant" (LOC 2016805972) is mostly a railroad bridge, so we left it out.
